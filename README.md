# Rugby World Cup

### Decription ###
My own interpretation of Rugby World Cup History included in simple application written in R Shiny.

### App ###
There are three tabs:  
- Participants - you choose Rugby World Cup Edition in filter and then you receive map of countries which took part in edition and position of every country,  
- History - table with the whole participants in RWC history and their achievements,  
- Comparison - you can choose from 2 to 4 countries and compare them according to their position in each of RWC edition.
